At Northfield Mount Hermon, we empower students to find their purpose as they stretch their intellect and deepen their compassion within a community that believes in their potential to serve and lead with their heads, hearts, and hands.

line

This is education for the head, heart, and hand.

Maeve Read my story.

Every part of my time at NMH has taught me that I can be a leader in my own way.

Maeve’s advice to new NMH students: Try new things, and don’t be afraid to make mistakes.

Unandi Read my story.

NMH has made me a more independent, kind, and knowledgeable person. The independence it granted me makes me feel prepared for the next steps in my life.

Excited to spread his wings at a boarding school, Unandi thrived as a scholar, a student leader, a musician, and an athlete.

Drema developed a passion for community service at NMH that she’ll carry with her wherever she goes.
